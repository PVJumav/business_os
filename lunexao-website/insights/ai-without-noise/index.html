AI and Emerging Technology Without the Noise

AI initiatives should be evaluated by business value, data readiness, user adoption and risk controls.

Insight

Useful AI starts with a practical business problem.

Organisations can avoid hype by focusing on use cases such as support triage, document review, reporting assistance, knowledge access and process recommendations.

Each use case should include data governance, approval controls, human review and clear success metrics.
