Digital Transformation

From Digitalisation to Digital Transformation

Digital files and online forms help, but transformation happens when the organisation changes how work flows and how decisions are made.

Insight

The goal is operational improvement.

Digitalisation converts information into digital formats. Transformation uses technology to improve processes, visibility, accountability and customer outcomes.

That shift requires process ownership, clear data structures, workflow design, leadership support and practical training for users.
