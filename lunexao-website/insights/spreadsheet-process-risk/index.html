Why Businesses Are Still Running Critical Processes on Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets are useful, but they become risky when they become the approval engine, audit trail and reporting system for critical work.

Insight

The issue is not the spreadsheet. It is the missing operating workflow.

Many organisations rely on spreadsheets because they are familiar, flexible and fast to start. Over time, the same files begin carrying requests, approvals, ownership, versions, status updates and management reports.

The cost appears later: duplicated data, missed approvals, weak accountability, slow reporting and decisions based on outdated files.

A practical transition starts by identifying the process behind the spreadsheet, defining the responsible roles, and moving repeatable steps into a workflow or business platform with audit history and useful dashboards.
