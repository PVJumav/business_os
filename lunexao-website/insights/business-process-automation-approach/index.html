Process Automation

How Organisations Should Approach Business Process Automation

Start with the work, not the tool. The best automation projects are specific, measurable and easy for teams to adopt.

Insight

Choose processes where rules, repetition and accountability are clear.

Good candidates include approvals, requests, document routing, customer onboarding, reporting cycles and internal service workflows.

Define ownership, required data, decision points, exceptions and metrics before implementation. That keeps automation practical and reduces expensive rework.
